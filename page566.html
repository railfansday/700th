#7791. Head of People at VRChat

📅 20 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

Join the VRChat Team

VRChat offers a first-of-its-kind, game-changing platform that provides an endless collection of social VR experiences and gives the power of creation to its robust community. With over 250,000 worlds and growing, VRChat’s vision is to allow users to bring their imaginations to life and help shape the metaverse anywhere in the world on any device. VRChat has raised $100M to date with the support of investors, Makers Fund, Anthos Capital and HTC. 

We have a great team which includes people from: Netflix, Twitter, Meta, Microsoft, Roblox, Google, Amazon, Unity, Spotify, Discord, Uber, eBay, Robinhood, Twitch, Zynga and TikTok. Come and join the mission!

Job Overview

Help VRChat build and sustain a small, exceptionally strong team. You'll lead a focused People function and work directly with leaders and managers to turn clear expectations into effective performance, development, and accountability practices. This is a hands-on leadership role: you'll build on existing infrastructure and partner closely with the recruiting lead.

We’re looking for a builder who combines strategic judgment with practical delivery, moves comfortably between strategy and hands-on work, and builds only the process the business needs. If you’re energized by leading high-impact environments and care deeply about building inclusive, community-aligned cultures, this role was built for you.

,

Job & Team Impact

As Head of People, you’ll shape how VRChat attracts, develops, and retains the talent that makes our mission possible. You’ll be a trusted partner to the CEO and business leaders, a coach to managers, and a champion for the people and culture that make VRChat distinct.
,

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Partner with senior leaders to strengthen leadership alignment, decision-making, and accountability. Challenge constructively, clarify decision rights, and move decisively once alignment is reached

  • Translate People priorities into practical deliverables with clear owners, milestones, and proactive communication of risks and trade-offs

  • Lead and develop the People team with clear priorities, support, and tools. Model calm, respectful leadership under pressure and handle sensitive matters with discretion, impartiality, and evidence-based judgment

  • Build trust in People as an approachable, reliable partner to employees and managers, with dependable core operations and onboarding

  • Run a practical performance-calibration and manager-accountability process that helps leaders recognize strong performance, identify gaps, support development, and address persistent underperformance promptly and fairly. Leaders own performance decisions; People equips, coaches, and challenges them

  • Coach managers through difficult feedback, employee relations issues, and organizational changes, balancing clear business expectations with fairness, sound judgment, and employee dignity

  • Use engagement, attrition, and performance insights to surface emerging organizational risks and capability gaps proactively, with practical recommendations and clear decisions for leadership

  • Lead the development and operation of leveling and compensation practices, including equity philosophy and communication to candidates and employees, in partnership with executive leadership and Finance

  • Use automation and AI where they demonstrably improve People operations, reduce manual work, or improve employee experience, with appropriate safeguards for sensitive employee data and human judgment

  • Partner closely with the recruiting lead on workforce planning, quality of hire, leveling, and candidate experience. Own the ongoing onboarding and employee lifecycle, using recruiting-to-ramp feedback to improve handoffs

  • Sustain and evolve VRChat's inclusive, remote-first culture across a globally distributed and diverse workforce

    • ,

    What Success Looks Like in Your First Year

  • Clearer expectations, consistent performance calibration, and managers better equipped to give feedback and address issues.

  • Strengthen talent density through growth and support. Managers consistently recognize and develop exceptional performers, identify performance gaps early, and provide clear expectations, actionable feedback, and meaningful support to help people improve with fair, timely decisions when progress falls short.

  • Reliable onboarding and core People operations, with clear owners, milestones, and early communication of risks.

  • A trusted, approachable People function that improves employee and manager experience with minimal unnecessary process.

  • Stronger leadership alignment and timely identification of organizational risks and capability gaps.

    • ,

    Experience, Skills & Qualifications

  • Demonstrated success leading a lean People function or a comparably broad People remit at a growing technology company, with direct ownership of performance practices, manager coaching, employee relations, and People operations

  • Demonstrated track record of building and executing people programs with measurable impact

  • Has guided a People function through meaningful inflection points, whether that's evolving how performance works, supporting the team through organizational change, investing in culture, or scaling through growth

  • Fast and iterative. Gets a v1 in front of stakeholders quickly and improves from there

  • Practical experience improving People workflows through automation or AI, with evidence of impact and sound judgment about privacy and human oversight

  • Able to communicate People insights, trade-offs, and recommendations clearly to senior leadership and turn discussion into decisions and action

  • Experience managing globally distributed, fully remote teams across multiple employment models

  • Empathetic and decisive; comfortable operating in ambiguity and making pragmatic calls with imperfect information

  • Aligned with how we work: bold enough to experiment, willing to engage in healthy debate and then commit fully, collaborative by default, and motivated by outcomes

    • ,

    Bonus Points

  • Background in tech, gaming, social platforms, or community-driven products; bonus if you understand that the product IS the community

  • Has worked at a company where the product is also a space the workforce inhabits (gaming, social platforms, creator tools) and understands the specific HR dynamics that come with that

  • Experience presenting People insights and recommendations to a board

  • A genuine track record of fostering belonging across a diverse, global workforce

  • You've spent time in VRChat, or you're curious about social VR and what it means for how people connect

    • ,

    Benefits

  • Work from anywhere! VRChat is a 100% remote company offering flexible working hours in combination with core hours supporting real-time collaboration

  • Health Benefits

  • 401K for US & Group RRSP for Canadian Employees

  • Stock Options

  • Generous paid holiday schedule

  • Unlimited/flexible vacation time

  • Paid parental leave benefits

    • VRChat is an equal-opportunity employer, and we welcome applicants from all backgrounds. VRChat fosters a diverse, creative, and collaborative environment where anyone can contribute to any of the ongoing projects or direction of the roadmap at any time.  If you’re a passionate team player who wants to have an impact on a dynamic team, we’d love to hear from you! 

    All job offers are subject to early stage ID checks, satisfactory referencing and background checks.

    #7792. Head of Communications (New Media) at Supabase

    📅 20 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

    About Supabase

    Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.

    About the Role

    Traditional PR is fading — we go directly to our audience. This role brings together what used to be two separate seats, communications and social media, into one mid-to-senior, hands-on role: owning Supabase's voice in the channels where developers actually pay attention, and building our founders' presence in them.

    "New media" here means modern and direct, not traditional press — we care far more about getting our founders onto the right podcasts and YouTube channels than landing a New York Times placement. It's part comms, part social operator, with a sharper edge than a typical polite PR hire: someone willing to put an opinion out there and push a little rather than always play it safe. But it's not just a social media job either — this person also needs the poise and technical credibility to lead comms through genuinely high-stakes moments, like a major incident, where reporters, investors, and customers all need a calm, credible, technically fluent voice at the same time.

    What You’ll Be Responsible For

    • Make sure we're part of the current conversation and are going directly to our audience — new media over old-world press

    • Own and execute our new media strategy

      • Run and grow Supabase's X + LinkedIn accounts, hands on keyboard

      • Help build the accounts of our founders and team — including ghostwriting in their voice

      • Explore and exploit unconventional channels and formats

      • Book our cofounders on the most known shows, podcasts, and YouTube channels in our space

    • Work directly with DevRel and the PMM team on launches and Select

    • Manage comms around incidents, funding, and other high-stakes moments — crisis comms handled with poise and technical credibility (this is key)

    You Might Be a Good Fit If You

    • Excellent writer with a distinctive voice — equally strong posting as yourself and ghostwriting for others

    • Track record of growing accounts, ideally for a technical audience

    • Unconventional thinker — willing to put an opinion out there and push a little, with the judgment to know when

    • Seasoned enough for the serious moments — incidents, funding, press — calm, credible, and technically fluent

    • High agency — you're probably already doing a version of this somewhere scrappier

    What We Offer

    • Fully Remote

      We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

    • ESOP

      Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

    • Tech Allowance

      Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

    • Health Benefits

      Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

    • Annual Off-Sites

      Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

    • Flexible Work

      We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

    • Professional Development

      Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

    About the Team

    Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

    • ~400 team members

    • 60+ countries

    • 20+ languages spoken

    • Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

    • 540,000+ community members

    We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.
