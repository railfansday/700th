#7810. Revenue Accounting Manager at LiveKit

📅 16 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

About LiveKit

LiveKit is building the infrastructure layer for the voice-driven era of computing. Our platform gives developers everything they need to build, test, deploy, scale, and observe agents in production. Founded in 2021, LiveKit powers voice AI applications for OpenAI, xAI, Salesforce, Coursera, Spotify, and thousands of others, collectively facilitating billions of calls each year.

About You

Our revenue is credit-denominated and consumption-based — usage billing, prepaid credit drawdown, and concurrency pricing across a growing enterprise base — which is close to the most complex revenue profile in infrastructure software. The load-bearing questions are open and there are few set policies and processes.

You will be the technical authority on revenue. You will own the entire Order to Cash cycle, from interfacing with our sales team, reviewing contracts, assessing terms in accordance with ASC 606, implementing workpapers and schedules, documenting conclusions, and assisting with billing and collection efforts. If building processes from square one and taking ownership of some of the most complex revenue arrangements excites you, this is the role for you.

You'll thrive as a Revenue Accounting Manager if you:

  • are obsessed with making the best product for our customers

  • are known as the go-to person for tackling tough problems

  • work hard and can build and ship fast

  • are focused on polish, detail and quality

  • are a fast learner, frequently picking up new tips, tricks, and skills

The best way to impress us is with thoughtful ways you'd implement LiveKit, and potentially tinkering with it 😊

What You'll Do

  • Author our revenue accounting policy set — principal versus agent on brokered inference, breakage on expiring credits, performance obligation identification and allocation, minimum commitment versus overage, and contract modifications — and make each one stand up to an external auditor

  • Own the monthly revenue close: recognition, the deferred revenue rollforward, contract asset and liability balances, and the flux analysis

  • Own the revenue portion of our external audit, from the PBC list through the technical positions

  • Replace manual revenue entries with an automated billing-to-revenue reconciliation and a documented exception process — revenue should be computed by a system and reviewed by a person, with invoice accuracy above 99% and root-cause tracking on the misses

  • Govern order to cash: billing accuracy, dispute resolution, and an AR and collections process with real DSO and aging targets

  • Own commission capitalization and amortization under ASC 340-40

  • Review non-standard deal structures before they're signed, so the accounting consequence is a decision rather than a discovery

Who You Are

  • You're a licensed CPA with a Big 4 or comparable technical accounting foundation, plus real industry experience in a high-growth startup environment

  • You have demonstrable ASC 606 experience with usage-based or consumption revenue

  • You write technical memos that a reviewer can follow without a meeting: facts, guidance, analysis, conclusion

  • Experience assessing GAAP guidance and implementing it in real-world situations

  • You've owned a monthly revenue close hands-on, and you've closed revenue through at least one external audit

  • You're comfortable being the technical authority without a bench behind you

Nice to Have

  • Billing system implementation or migration — Metronome, Stripe, Zuora

  • Pre-IPO or first-audit experience

  • Enough SQL to validate a recognition schedule against raw usage data yourself

Our Commitment to You

  • An opportunity to build something truly impactful to the world

  • Contribute to open source alongside world-class engineers

  • Competitive salary and equity package

  • Health, dental, and vision benefits

  • Flexible vacation policy

LiveKit is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate on the basis of any characteristic protected by applicable law. If you require a reasonable accommodation during the application or interview process, please contact recruiting@livekit.io.

#7811. Customer Growth Specialist at Uscreen

📅 16 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

What we're doing at Uscreen

Uscreen is the video monetization platform for businesses building recurring revenue from video content. Our customers replace a patchwork stack of separate hosting, community, courses, email, and app vendors with one branded experience across web, mobile, and TV.

The platform is only half of it. We believe growth requires behavior, not just software. Customers don't stall because a feature is missing. They stall because they priced wrong, never built an email list, or launched an offer nobody wanted. Coaching is how we fix that, and it's part of the product rather than an add-on.

The job

You own a segment.

We have a cohort of smaller video businesses where retention is not where it should be. Nobody owns it today. You will. That means the revenue that stays in that segment, and the revenue that grows inside it, is yours. Your lemonade stand, your numbers, your call on how to run it.

This is a coaching role measured in dollars. You'll be accountable for gross revenue retention in your cohort first, and expansion second. Not tickets closed, not calls booked.

It's also a one-to-many job. Your cohort is larger than a 1:1 book, so the work is designing coaching that scales: cohort programs, group sessions, office hours, triggered outreach when the data says a business is drifting. You'll build that motion rather than inherit it.

What you'll do

  • Own gross revenue retention and expansion for an assigned segment of customers
  • Find the pattern in why businesses in your cohort stall, and build coaching that addresses it at scale rather than one conversation at a time
  • Run group coaching sessions, cohort programs, and asynchronous coaching inside Membership+
  • Coach customers through the decisions that move revenue: pricing, packaging, positioning, offer design, email and funnel strategy, retention mechanics
  • Read your own data. Spot the accounts drifting before they churn, and know which interventions actually worked
  • Build the playbooks and session formats that make this repeatable. You're the first person in this seat, and what you build becomes how we scale it
  • Bring what you learn back to Product and Success. You'll be closer to this segment than anyone at the company.

Who we're looking for

Someone who has grown something online and can explain how. Maybe you ran marketing for a small business, coached founders, built your own audience, or worked inside a membership or subscription business. What matters is that you can sit with an operator, see the real constraint, and say the honest thing.

  • 3 to 5 years in coaching, consulting, customer success, or digital marketing, with real ownership of an outcome
  • Comfortable advising solo operators and small teams on how to grow
  • Strong live facilitation: group sessions, workshops, webinars, 1:1 calls
  • Can read a dashboard and form a point of view on retention curves, cohort behavior, and funnel conversion
  • Working fluency with AI tools for research, prep, and synthesis. Our team uses Claude daily to prep sessions, analyze customer data, and build coaching assets. You don't need to be technical, but you should reach for these tools rather than around them.
  • Motivated by being paid on results. A third of this package is variable and there's no ceiling on it.
  • Clear, simple communication. Complex idea in, plain sentence out.

Bonus points if you:

  • Have been a creator, coach, or small business owner yourself
  • Have worked with video businesses, online educators, or subscription and membership businesses
  • Know the surrounding stack: Kit/ConvertKit, YouTube, Kajabi, Circle, Squarespace, Meta and IG
  • Have built a scaled or one-to-many customer program before

What's hard about this job

We'd rather you know now.

  • You'll own a number you can't fully control. Some customers won't take the advice and will churn anyway. You have to be able to own influence over an outcome rather than the outcome itself.
  • It's a lagging metric. Retention moves slowly. Your first two quarters are guaranteed at target because of this, but you'll be doing the work for months before the number reflects it.
  • The motion doesn't exist yet. Playbooks exist for our 1:1 coaching. The scaled version for this segment is an open question you'll help answer. If you need a finished playbook on day one, this will frustrate you.
  • You'll deliver honest assessments to people emotionally invested in what they've built. That takes a specific kind of nerve.

What we offer

  • 100% remote first company
  • Flexible PTO + 8 company holidays
  • Remote, global team
  • Paid parental leave
  • Medical, dental, and vision (US Based)
  • Technology and home office stipend
  • Coworking space stipend
  • Annual learning stipend

Note: Benefits may vary depending on location and local regulations. Final eligibility and coverage details will be shared during the hiring process.
