#35691. Teen arrested in Florida shooting will be tried as adult, US officials say

📅 27 Oct 2026 | Category: technology | Source

Sign up now: Get ST's newsletters delivered to your inbox

The shooting took place in Perry Street near Florida A&M University.

Tallahassee, Florida – A 15-year-old boy who was arrested in the shooting of 11 people after a college football game in Tallahassee, Florida, will stand trial as an adult, a Florida prosecutor said on Oct 26.

The teenager, Anthony Rivers III, was ordered to remain in custody at a juvenile court hearing on Oct 26 after his arrest the day before. The shooting took place in a crowded, closed-off street on Oct 24 about an hour after Florida A&M University’s (FAMU) homecoming game. A crowd of just over 20,000 attended the event.

The shooting left six people hospitalised as of Oct 25, one of whom was in critical condition. A spokesperson for Leon County School District said four of its students were wounded, including the one in critical condition.

Six of the 11 people shot were minors, the Tallahassee Police Department said. A 12th person was injured but not shot, officials said.

Lawrence Revell, chief of the Tallahassee Police, said at a news conference on Oct 26 that the shooting appeared to have stemmed from a physical altercation among people who knew one another and that none of the victims appeared to be intended targets. He said more arrests were possible.

Judge Andrew Sheeran, who presided over the hearing on Oct 26, said he would grant the request of the top prosecutor in Tallahassee to try Rivers as an adult, which Florida law allows in certain cases. He will be charged with 11 counts of attempted second-degree murder and one count of possession of a firearm by a delinquent, officials said.

“It’s our discretion because of the severity of the offence,” said prosecutor Jack Campbell.

In a phone interview, he added that he had discussed the decision with juvenile-case prosecutors before asking his prosecutors to review the case. “Ultimately, it was my decision,” he said.

Rivers, dressed in a white T-shirt, grey gym shorts and sandals and his hair pulled up into a bun, stood quietly at the hearing on Oct 26 with his hands clasped behind his back as the public defender who was representing him addressed the court.

The public defender said she had previously represented Rivers. Her office did not reply to a request for comment. Efforts to reach relatives of Rivers were not immediately successful.

According to court records, Rivers has been arrested previously on charges including burglary and theft of a gun, but it was unclear what the outcome was. He was arrested on Sept 6 for violating his probation and had been sentenced to a juvenile home.

According to a police affidavit, Rivers was arrested after surveillance cameras and an ankle monitor he was wearing identified him at a Tallahassee mall where gunfire had erupted in an underground carpark.

The police identified another 15-year-old who was with Rivers as the shooter, according to the affidavit. Nobody was injured in the shooting, which occurred after an altercation in the mall.

Campbell declined to discuss anything about Rivers’ criminal record. But he said if someone was a repeat offender, “that’s something we consider” when determining whether to charge a juvenile as an adult.

The shooting echoed others in Tallahassee in recent years. Nine people were shot – one fatally – outside a liquor store in 2022 that Campbell said was connected to a homecoming event at Florida A&M.

In 2025, two people were killed and six others were wounded in a shooting at Florida State University, which is near Florida A&M.

Campbell is seeking the death penalty against the shooter in that case, Phoenix Ikner, who has pleaded not guilty and is awaiting trial.

The shooting on Oct 24 could have been much worse, Campbell said.

It happened in Perry Street, a residential street that runs alongside Bragg Memorial Stadium, where the football team plays. The street was blocked off for vendors selling food and T-shirts, and is a popular place before and after games.

“If you go to FAMU, you’ve spent time in Perry Street,” Campbell said. NYTIMES

#35949. Donald Trump Accused of Rape in Report Linking President to Jeffrey Epstein

📅 27 Oct 2026 | Category: technology | Source

President Donald Trump is being accused of historic rape allegations by a former beauty queen as part of a House Judiciary Committee report on the former reality TV star’s alleged ties to the late convicted sex trafficker Jeffrey Epstein.

Rep. Jamie Raskin, the ranking Democratic member of the House Judiciary Committee, released a shocking report on Monday, October 26, that alleges that Trump, 80, was a “co-conspirator” in “Jeffrey Epstein’s conspiracy to traffic and abuse young women in the 1990s,” in addition to being accused by more than 25 women of sexual impropriety throughout his lifetime.

The president has denied ever committing sexual assault. Trump has previously claimed he ended his friendship with Epstein and kicked him out of his Palm Beach resort Mar-A-Lago over allegations the disgraced billionaire “stole” his employees — long before Epstein was convicted of procuring a person under 18 for prostitution and soliciting prostitution in 2008.

Committee staffers spoke with former Swiss beauty queen Beatrice Keul, who previously alleged in a 2024 Daily Mail interview that Trump groped her during an encounter at New York City’s Plaza Hotel in November 1993.

Keul, 55, subsequently told the House committee that she met Trump at the Plaza Hotel in connection with the American Dream Pageant. Once Trump stepped away from their conversation, Epstein — who died by suicide in 2019 after being arrested on fresh sex trafficking charges — allegedly introduced himself to Keul as “Don’s best friend” and offered to arrange her travel to Mar-A-Lago.

The former model further alleges that Trump later raped her in his hotel room, promising to help her arrange a visa and secure her enrollment New York University if she did not go public about the alleged encounter. He later allegedly reiterated his promise when he called her in Switzerland sometime later.

“Upon entering [his hotel room], Trump reportedly grabbed Keul from behind and forcibly raped her; in the process, tearing her dress and causing bleeding and bruising on multiple parts of her body,” the Judiciary Committee report read. “Keul described the rape as ‘brutal.'”

The House report contains direct testimony from four other women who “accused Trump of rape or sexual assault in Epstein’s company or presence.”

“None of these allegations are true. This ‘report’ is nothing more than a desperate attempt by crazed Democrats to distract Americans from the surging Republican momentum driven by President Trump’s historic achievements,” White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis told Us on Monday and as the White House remains without a press secretary. “The President kicked Jeffrey Epstein out of his club at Mar-a-Lago because he was a creep, and he has been fully exonerated of anything related to Epstein.”

Following the publication of the House Judiciary Committee report on Monday, Rep. Raskin, 63, called for the appointment of an independent special counsel to investigate Trump’s alleged ties to Epstein in greater detail.

“Donald Trump says he knew Jeffrey Epstein ‘just like everybody in Palm Beach knew him.’ Not exactly. Evidence involving 11 women tells a completely different and far more damning story about their relationship and the abuse of young women and girls that took place in their network,” Rep. Raskin said in a statement. “Five women have directly accused Trump of rape or sexual assault in Epstein’s company or presence; others have indicated that Epstein or [his accomplice Ghislaine] Maxwell attempted to traffic them to Trump.”

Rep. Raskin alleged that although multiple witnesses and alleged victims have “spoken to investigators and, in some cases, testified under oath,” Trump’s Justice Department has allegedly “shut down investigations, deliberately withheld evidence and, through [Attorney General] Todd Blanche, prevented Ghislaine Maxwell from discussing a survivor whose account implicated Trump — all while insisting there is no basis upon which to investigate Epstein’s possible accomplices.”

“Trump’s Justice Department appears to be covering up these crimes to protect the President,” the committee’s ranking Democrat continued. “The women who survived Epstein deserve to be heard, Congress must have the complete files required by law, and an independent special counsel must investigate Epstein’s co-conspirators and DOJ’s cover-up and ensure that state and foreign prosecutors have the evidence they need to pursue justice.”

In addition to his alleged ties to Epstein, Trump has been accused of sexual impropriety by more than two dozen women.

Writer E. Jean Carroll successfully sued Trump amid accusations of defamation and sexual assault over an alleged incident that occurred in the 1990s in a Bergdorf Goodman department store. Carroll, 82, was awarded $5 million and $83.3 million in damages in two respective judgments. (The Supreme Court denied Trump’s request for the high court to review his appeal of the $5 million judgment in August.)

The 1993 book Lost Tycoon: The Many Lives of Donald Trump included allegations that the mogul committed a “violent assault” against his ex-wife Ivana Trump during their marriage. (Trump called the accusations “obviously false” at the time.)

“[O]n one occasion during 1989, Mr. Trump and I had marital relations in which he behaved very differently toward me than he had during our marriage,” Ivana later said in a statement. “As a woman, I felt violated, as the love and tenderness, which he normally exhibited towards me, was absent. I referred to this as a ‘rape,’ but I do not want my words to be interpreted in a literal or criminal sense.”

Ivana was granted a divorce from Donald in December 1990 on the grounds of “cruel and inhumane” conduct because of his affair with eventual second wife Marla Maples. (Donald and Maples, 62, were married from 1993 to 1999 and he later wed Melania Trump in 2005. Ivana died at age 73 in 2022.)

#35596. Syria closes border crossing with Jordan after airstrikes

📅 27 Oct 2026 | Category: technology | Source

BEIRUT, October 27. /TASS/. Syria’s General Authority of Borders and Customs has closed the Nasib border crossing with Jordan to passenger and freight transport, the agency said in a statement on X.

"We announce the temporary closure of the border crossing due to the situation in the Daraa Governorate," the statement reads. "Passenger and commercial traffic has been suspended in both directions to ensure the safety of travelers and border service personnel," the agency added.

Earlier, the Syrian Foreign Ministry said it had notified Amman of the decision, citing Jordan’s failure to coordinate its operations in Syria.

"We express deep regret over civilian casualties in Daraa caused by Jordanian airstrikes," the statement added.

On October 26, the Jordanian Air Force carried out strikes in southern Syria, targeting extremist groups reportedly linked to the terrorists behind a recent attack on St. George’s Orthodox Church in Jordan’s Al-Karak Governorate. According to Jordan’s Health Ministry, the October 23 terrorist attack killed nine people and injured 34 others.
