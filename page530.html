#7588. HubSpot : Lead Counsel, Public Company Governance & Corporate Programs

📅 21 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

Headquarters: United States
URL: https://www.hubspot.com/

Lead Counsel, Public Company Governance & Corporate Programs

Legal

Remote - USA

POS-20140

The Legal Team

HubSpot's Legal team is a proactive, business-enabling partner — not a back-office function. We help our teams move fast, smart, and responsibly, balancing innovation with rigor so HubSpot can scale as a publicly traded, AI-native company while maintaining trust, compliance, and clarity.

Role Summary

This is a role for a senior governance attorney who has earned the confidence of boards and committees, not just supported them. You will be HubSpot's primary governance lawyer — owning the company's governance policy framework, the NG&S Committee, the operational backbone for all three Board committees, proxy governance and annual meeting, and governance-focused investor engagement. 

The governance attorney who succeeds here doesn't just maintain recurring programs — they actively improve how those programs work, bringing the same rigor to process design as to legal advice. 

HubSpot is building an AI-first legal function, and we want someone who sees that as an opportunity to do governance work at a higher level of quality and scale, not as an obligation to satisfy.

This is an individual contributor role reporting to the Director of Corporate Legal.

What You'll Do

1. Lead HubSpot's Governance Policy Framework

  • Own HubSpot's governance policy framework, including committee charters, governance practices, director independence analysis, related-party frameworks, and Board refresh and skills matrix legal analysis.
  • Lead the annual governance policy refresh, ensuring HubSpot's practices remain aligned with evolving NYSE requirements and governance best practices — and anticipating changes before they arrive.

2.  Own the Nominating, Governance & Sustainability Committee

  • Serve as the substantive legal owner for the NG&S Committee, preparing materials, advising on agenda items, translating legal and governance requirements into practical recommendations, and supporting the committee chair.
  • Advise on director independence, related-party considerations, Board refresh, and skills matrix analysis, with the judgment to resolve most questions independently.

3. Run Board and Committee Operations

  • Own the operational backbone for the Board and its Audit, Compensation, and NG&S Committees as a shared service across all three committees.
  • Own the governance sections of HubSpot's annual proxy statement, including director biographies, skills matrix, committee descriptions, governance policy disclosures, and ESG and sustainability content, and lead the annual meeting process and logistics end-to-end.

4. Lead Governance-Focused Investor Engagement

  • Lead governance-focused investor engagement, including proxy advisor outreach related to Board composition and governance practices, and partner with Investor Relations on shareholder communications and proxy season strategy.
  • Engage directly with ISS, Glass Lewis, and significant shareholders on governance matters with the preparation and credibility to represent HubSpot's positions authoritatively.

5. Own Corporate Governance Programs

  • Own recurring corporate governance programs (governance filings, D&O questionnaires, governance surveys, and related compliance obligations) with disciplined execution and strong records practice.

6. Build an AI-First Governance Function

  • You have a point of view on how AI and automation can raise the quality and efficiency of recurring governance work, and you act on it. You will identify and implement process improvements (including AI-enabled workflows) that reduce friction, strengthen governance operations, and increase the team's operating leverage.
  • Build templates, playbooks, and governance infrastructure that make this function more scalable and resilient over time.

What You'll Bring

Required Qualifications

  • 8-10+ years of corporate and securities law experience at top law firms and/or in-house, with at least 3-4 years of meaningful in-house experience advising a U.S. public company on corporate governance matters.
  • Deep knowledge of public company governance (Board and committee practices, director independence, governance policies, stock exchange requirements, and related-party frameworks) with the judgment to own governance calls independently.
  • Extensive experience supporting Boards and Board committees, with the credibility to advise on governance matters directly and translate legal requirements into practical, boardroom-ready recommendations.
  • Meaningful ownership of, or a senior role in, annual proxy statement preparation and annual shareholder meeting processes.
  • Demonstrated track record of governance-related investor and proxy advisor engagement; able to partner with Investor Relations and senior stakeholders as a peer, not a process manager.
  • Track record of independently managing complex, recurring governance programs with strong attention to legal judgment, execution, and records discipline — and a drive to continuously improve how those programs run.
  • JD from an accredited law school and active bar membership in at least one U.S. jurisdiction.

Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Experience working in-house at a publicly traded technology or SaaS company.
  • Experience engaging directly with proxy advisors, including ISS and Glass Lewis, on governance matters.
  • Experience advising on ESG or sustainability-related governance and public disclosures.
  • Hands-on experience using or building AI-enabled workflows in legal or governance contexts, with a clear point of view on where AI adds real leverage in governance work.

Where You'll Work

  • Location: United States
  • Work location preference eligibility: Remote
  • Travel/Shift: No travel or shift requirements

Application Requirements

  • There is no application deadline for this role.
  • Applications and resumes/CVs must be submitted in English.
  • Cover letters are not required.

Pay & Benefits

The cash compensation below includes base salary, on-target commission for employees in eligible roles, and annual bonus targets under HubSpot’s bonus plan for eligible roles. In addition to cash compensation, some roles are eligible to participate in HubSpot’s equity plan to receive restricted stock units (RSUs). Some roles may also be eligible for overtime pay. Individual compensation packages are tailored to your skills, experience, qualifications, and other job-related reasons.

At HubSpot, fair compensation practices aren’t just about checking off the box for legal compliance. It’s about living out our value of transparency with our employees, candidates, and community.

$165,600—$265,000 USD

At HubSpot, we value both flexibility and connection. Whether you’re a Remote employee or work from the Office, we want you to start your journey here by building strong connections with your team and peers. If you are joining our Engineering team, you will be required to attend a regional HubSpot office for in-person onboarding. If you join our broader Product team, you’ll also attend other in-person events, such as your Product Group Summit and other gatherings, to continue building on those connections.

#7783. GRC Analyst at Supabase

📅 21 Oct 2026 | Category: other | Source

About the Role

We’re looking for a GRC Analyst to join our Compliance & Security team and help make risk management, audit and compliance a continuous part of how Supabase runs. You’ll work closely with engineering, security, IT, and legal to help us define our risk tolerance, keep our compliance programs (including SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022) current, test controls on a rolling basis, and answer customer and vendor security reviews quickly and accurately.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in async, fast-paced environments and is excited about building developer tools that scale to millions.

What You’ll Be Responsible For

  • Run risk assessments across the company and major product changes. Score likelihood and impact, propose treatments, and bring the results to leadership to inform risk tolerance decisions.

  • Maintain the risk register, track remediation to closure, and report status to compliance and security leadership.

  • Monitor controls on a rolling basis, using automated tests where they exist and manual testing where they don’t. Triage failures, prioritize by the risk register, and align each finding with an owner and a due date.

  • Support internal audit, whether performed by an external partner or in-house: propose scope from the risk register, supply evidence, and track findings to closure.

  • Run day-to-day operations for our compliance programs: evidence collection, control owner follow-up, and readiness ahead of each audit period.

  • Extend continuous control validation beyond what our compliance platform covers. Build custom tests with AI coding tools, work with engineering and IT to pull evidence from source systems instead of collecting screenshots, and check that each test proves what the control requires.

You Might Be a Good Fit If You

  • Have 3+ years of experience in GRC, internal audit, or security compliance at a SaaS or technology company

  • Are comfortable working with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls, a compliance platform such as Drata, Vanta, and evidence from systems like AWS, GCP, Google Workspace, and Okta

  • Communicate clearly across both technical and non-technical audiences

  • Have experience in async or globally distributed teams

  • Are energized by solving real-world problems for developers

  • Are comfortable navigating ambiguity and moving quickly

What We Offer

  • Fully Remote

    We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

  • ESOP

    Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

  • Tech Allowance

    Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

  • Health Benefits

    Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

  • Annual Off-Sites

    Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

  • Flexible Work

    We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

  • Professional Development

    Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

About the Team

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

  • ~400 team members

  • 60+ countries

  • 20+ languages spoken

  • Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

  • 540,000+ community members

We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.
